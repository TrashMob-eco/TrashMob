## What TrashMob provides

- **Event coordination** — Volunteers create and publicize cleanup events; waivers, attendee lists, and hauler notifications are handled automatically.
- **Adoptable areas** — Streets, parks, beaches, or traffic islands that groups or businesses can claim and maintain over time (mirrors "Adopt-a-Spot" style programs).
- **Litter reporting** — Mobile app lets residents geotag litter with photos; feeds a work queue for city crews or volunteer groups.
- **Branded community page** — {City}-branded landing page with your logo, colors, and calendar of local events.
- **City impact reports** — Bags collected, hours volunteered, areas cleaned — exportable for Sustainable CT / grant / council reporting.
