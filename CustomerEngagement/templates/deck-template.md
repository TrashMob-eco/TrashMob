## What TrashMob provides — today

| Capability | How it helps {City} |
|---|---|
| Event creation & registration | Volunteers self-organize; you don't chase RSVPs |
| Digital waivers | Legal coverage, adult and minor (13+) workflows |
| Hauling partner coordination | Auto-notify pickup crews with photos + GPS |
| Mobile litter reporting | Residents geotag + photo; feeds a work queue |
| Adoptable areas | Groups/businesses claim streets, parks, islands |
| City impact reports | Bags, hours, areas — exportable for reporting |

## What a partnership could look like

- **Community page setup** — {City}-branded landing page, adoptable areas, waivers
- **First co-branded event** — pick a visible location; we help staff it
- **Volunteer recruitment support** — onboarding, training, event-day playbook
- **Impact reporting** — bags, hours, areas, per-attendee metrics, official PDFs

We'll shape scope and cadence with you — no one-size-fits-all playbook here.
