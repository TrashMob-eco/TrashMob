# TrashMob for Norwalk
**Mobilize volunteers to clean up Norwalk. Free, non-profit, ready to pilot in 90 days.**

---

## Norwalk today
Norwalk earned **Sustainable CT Gold Certification** and **Climate Leader** status in 2025 — one of only 12 gold cities in the state. The Office of Sustainability and Resilience is advancing the **2024 Sustainability & Resilience Plan**, and the **Green Workforce Program** launched in April 2026 is planting up to 200 native trees. Volunteer cleanup coordination, though, is still ad hoc: **Adopt-a-Spot** covers only traffic islands, and general litter/park cleanups flow through a single Neighborhood Improvement Coordinator by phone. There's no shared event calendar, no digital waivers, no citizen litter reporting, and no impact metrics feeding back into Sustainable CT reporting.

## What TrashMob provides — today, in production

- **Norwalk-branded community page** — your logo, colors, tagline, and calendar of local events on a dedicated Norwalk page. Regional rollup to Fairfield County or CT-wide is also available.
- **Adopt-A-Location** — residents, teams, and businesses claim streets, parks, beaches, or traffic islands to maintain over time. A natural extension of Norwalk's Adopt-a-Spot program, with an AI-assisted area editor so setup takes minutes, not weeks.
- **Sponsored adoptions** — local businesses can fund professional cleanup on a stretch they've adopted, with dashboards showing volunteer vs. sponsored impact.
- **Event coordination** — residents create and publicize cleanups; digital waivers (community-specific), attendee lists, hauler notifications, and event weather forecast are all handled.
- **"Start a Pick" (Instant Events)** — solo, Strava-style logging for casual pickers on a walk. Every bag counts toward Norwalk's metrics.
- **Mobile litter reporting** — residents geotag litter with photos from iOS/Android; feeds a prioritized work queue the Green Workforce crew or volunteer groups can act on.
- **Impact tracking** — per-attendee metrics, before/after photos, route recording with **litter density heatmap** (web + backend live; mobile route recording under device testing), official participation reports (PDF) for school/court/employer verification.
- **Trust & safety** — Waivers V3 (community-specific), PRIVO parental consent for minors (production-live), photo moderation, leaderboards, event co-leads.

## Fit with Norwalk's plan

- **Sustainable CT actions** — every documented cleanup, adopted area, and volunteer hour is a countable action toward maintaining Gold + Climate Leader status through 2028.
- **Green Workforce amplifier** — volunteer cleanups on weekends extend paid-crew reach without replacing them; citizen litter reports feed the crew's priority list.
- **Coastal + flood resilience** — storm-drain and shoreline cleanups (Calf Pasture Beach, Sheffield Island, drain outfalls) tie directly to flood-mitigation goals — not just aesthetics.
