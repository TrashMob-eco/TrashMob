# TrashMob for Norwalk
### Mobilize volunteers to clean up the city
Free · Non-profit · Open source · Live in production since 2022

Presented to **Jodi Trendler** — Director of Sustainability and Resilience, City of Norwalk

---

## Who we are

- **TrashMob.eco** — US **501(c)(3) non-profit**, launched May 2022
- Platform for organizing community cleanup events at scale
- Web + iOS + Android — all **open source** on GitHub
- Built by volunteers, funded by donations
- **No licensing fees. Ever.**

---

## Norwalk today — what you've built

- **Sustainable CT Gold Certification** + **Climate Leader** (2025) — one of only 12 gold cities in the state
- **2024 Sustainability & Resilience Plan** actively rolling out
- **Green Workforce Program** — up to 200 native trees, paid job training (April 2026)
- **School composting** launched Jan 2026
- **Adopt-a-Spot** — traffic islands, run by Keep Norwalk Beautiful

---

## Where volunteer cleanups get stuck today

- Adopt-a-Spot covers **traffic islands only** — streets, parks, and beaches have no equivalent
- General cleanups flow through **one coordinator by phone** (203-854-7978)
- **No digital waivers** — legal risk on every event
- **No citizen litter reporting** — no geotagged work queue for the Green Workforce crew
- **No impact data captured** — bags, hours, and areas cleaned aren't documented for Sustainable CT recertification

---

## What TrashMob provides — today, in production

| Capability | How it helps Norwalk |
|---|---|
| **Norwalk-branded community page** | Your logo, colors, calendar — a proper city presence |
| **Adopt-A-Location** | Streets, parks, beaches, islands — extends Adopt-a-Spot |
| **Sponsored adoptions** | Local businesses fund pro cleanup on adopted stretches |
| **Community-specific waivers** | Adult live · minor infra live · PRIVO parental consent live |
| **Mobile litter reporting** | Residents geotag; Green Workforce crew prioritizes |
| **"Start a Pick" (Instant Events)** | Strava-style solo logging on a walk |
| **Impact tracking** | Per-attendee metrics · before/after photos · density heatmap |
| **Official participation reports** | PDF for school / court / employer verification |

---

## Why this fits Norwalk specifically

**Sustainable CT actions add up**
Every documented cleanup, adopted area, and volunteer hour counts toward maintaining Gold + Climate Leader status through 2028.

**Green Workforce amplifier, not replacement**
Weekend volunteers extend paid-crew reach; citizen litter reports feed the crew's priority list.

**Coastal + flood resilience**
Storm-drain and shoreline cleanups (Calf Pasture Beach, Sheffield Island, drain outfalls) support the flood-mitigation narrative.

*All metrics captured automatically today — sample 90-day pilot output:*

- 42 cleanup events · 12 "Start a Pick" solo picks
- 380 volunteers · 1,240 volunteer-hours · per-attendee breakdown
- 720 bags collected · ~14,400 lbs of litter (weights tracked)
- 18 adopted street segments · 6 adopted parks · 3 sponsored stretches
- Before/after photos · litter density heatmap over covered areas
- Top-covered areas: South Norwalk, East Norwalk shoreline, Norwalk Green
- Ready-to-attach appendix for **Sustainable CT recertification**
- Official **participation reports (PDF)** available per volunteer
